# Helat Player

**Learn a language from the films you love.** Helat Player is an Android video player that
turns the lines you hear into flashcards: the audio, a frame of the scene, the sentence and
its meaning. Review them a few minutes a day with a 5-box Leitner system.

**یادگیری زبان با فیلم.** Helat Player پخش‌کننده‌ی ویدیوی اندروید است که جمله‌هایی را که
در فیلم می‌شنوید به کارت تبدیل می‌کند: صدا، تصویری از صحنه، جمله و معنی‌اش. روزی چند دقیقه
با روش لایتنر مرورشان کنید.

## Features

- Plays files and direct links in every format VLC supports, including HEVC 10-bit MKV.
- Save a line with one tap while the film keeps playing; exact times and text from the
  subtitles, the meaning from subtitles in your own language.
- Choose the language you learn and your own; load a second subtitle file for meanings.
- Review cards that turn over and are swiped, with listen-first, pronunciation practice and
  a daily streak.
- Line by line navigation, loop, speed, subtitle search and subtitle sync.
- Role-play: speak one side of a scene with the film.
- Background listening, daily reminder, backup and restore.
- Four themes, light and dark; English, Persian and Kurdish (Sorani).

## Privacy

Everything stays on your phone: cards, recordings and settings. The app sends nothing
anywhere; it only connects to the links you open.
